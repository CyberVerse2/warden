# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Developers and agent-platform teams building autonomous agents that need to pay for APIs, RPC, data, compute, storage, or model calls. They are evaluating whether Warden is credible enough to sit between agent runtimes and real money.

## Product Purpose

Warden is a programmable policy, custody, and audit layer for agentic x402 payments on Solana. It lets developers give agents wallets with rules, enforce policy before every spend, sign only approved payments, and record receipts for every autonomous request.

The landing page should make the authority split obvious in one glance: agents get spending capability; operators keep control.

## Brand Personality

Precise, restrained, operational. Warden should feel like serious infrastructure: a control plane for money-moving agents, not a crypto campaign page or generic AI tooling site.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI-agent visuals, robots, glowing brains, crypto confetti, dashboard-card sprawl, oversized SaaS hero metrics, and decorative security-shield fluff. Avoid explaining every implementation detail before the visitor understands the core primitive.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the primitive: wallets for agents, spend under policy.
2. Show the control loop instead of narrating it repeatedly.
3. Make trust visible through receipts, policy decisions, and scoped custody.
4. Keep diagrams operational, not decorative.
5. Hide deep protocol detail until the visitor asks for it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks, visible focus states, text alternatives for meaningful imagery, strong contrast on the dark interface, and reduced reliance on color-only status. Motion should remain subtle and non-essential.
